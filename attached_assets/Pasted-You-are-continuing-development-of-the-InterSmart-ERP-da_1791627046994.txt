==================================================
1. LEAD
==================================================

A lead represents a potential business opportunity before it becomes a confirmed project/customer relationship.

Fields:

- id UUID primary key
- leadCode unique
- customerId nullable
- assignedToId
- source
- contactName
- email nullable
- phone nullable
- companyName nullable
- requirement nullable
- estimatedValue Decimal nullable
- expectedCloseDate nullable
- status
- temperature
- notes nullable
- createdAt
- updatedAt
- deletedAt nullable

RELATIONSHIPS:

Lead
 ├── Customer nullable
 ├── Assigned Sales User
 └── Lead Activities

`assignedToId` must reference the existing User model.

`customerId` must reference the existing Customer model.

IMPORTANT:

A lead can exist before a customer record exists.

Therefore:

customerId MUST be nullable.

==================================================
IMPORTANT DATA RULES
==================================================

1. Use UUID primary keys.

2. Use PostgreSQL.

3. Use Prisma.

4. Use Decimal for estimatedValue.

5. Do not store calculated conversion percentages.

6. Do not store dashboard counts.

7. Dashboard metrics will be calculated from lead data.

8. Do not duplicate user information inside Lead.

9. Do not duplicate customer information unnecessarily.

10. Use foreign keys.

11. Add appropriate indexes.

12. Use createdAt and updatedAt appropriately.

13. Use deletedAt only where appropriate.

14. Do not use organizationId.

15. Do not use tenantId.

16. Do not add AWS dependencies.

17. Do not introduce unnecessary JSONB fields.
