PROJECT STATUS
==================================================

Important architectural rule:

Do NOT treat every business reason as a completely separate project status.

Separate:

1. Project lifecycle status
2. Project status reason

Create the main ProjectStatus enum:

PLANNED
ONGOING
ON_HOLD
COMPLETED
TERMINATED

The project may also have a status reason.

Create a ProjectStatusReason enum:

PAYMENT_PENDING
NO_CLIENT_RESPONSE
OUT_OF_SCOPE
CONTENT_PENDING
IMAGE_PENDING
PAYMENT_GATEWAY_PENDING
SMS_PENDING
SMTP_PENDING
TOO_MANY_CORRECTIONS
REPEATED_CORRECTIONS
BUDGET_EXCEEDED
DATE_EXCEEDED
MEETING_PENDING
APPROVAL_PENDING
OTHER

If a better normalized design is needed, document it before changing the architecture.

Do NOT make statuses like:

PAYMENT_PENDING
BUDGET_EXCEEDED
DATE_EXCEEDED

the main lifecycle status.

Those are reasons/conditions.

==================================================
PROPOSAL RELATIONSHIP
==================================================

Project.proposalId is nullable.

A project may be created from an accepted proposal.

Do not enforce the complete proposal status → project creation workflow at database level.

That belongs in the backend service layer.

Do not create a reverse "projectId" field in Proposal unless it is genuinely required.

Avoid circular or duplicated relationships.

==================================================
